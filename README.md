# Online Food Ordering System for a Single Restaurant

## Project Description

The Online Food Ordering System is a web-based application designed for a single restaurant to manage its food menu, customer orders, and online ordering process efficiently. Customers can browse available food items, view prices and descriptions, add items to their cart, and place orders online without visiting the restaurant physically.

The system includes an **Admin Panel** that allows restaurant staff to manage food items, update prices, track customer orders, and change order statuses such as Pending, Preparing, Ready, and Delivered.

The application provides a user-friendly and responsive interface that works on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices. It helps reduce manual work, minimize ordering errors, and improve the overall customer experience.

## Key Features

- **Customer Registration and Login:** Secure account creation and login.
- **Food Menu:** Display food items with images, descriptions, prices, and categories.
- **Shopping Cart:** Add, remove, and update food quantities.
- **Online Ordering:** Place orders with customer details and delivery or pickup options.
- **Order Tracking:** View order status in real time or through regular status updates.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Manage menu items, categories, prices, and orders.
- **Order Management:** Accept, reject, and update orders.
- **Payment Options:** Support Cash on Delivery and online payment integration, if configured.
- **Responsive Design:** Accessible on mobile phones, tablets, and computers.
- **Database Management:** Store customer details, menu items, orders, and payment records securely.

## Objective

The main objective of this project is to develop a convenient and efficient online food ordering platform for a single restaurant, enabling customers to order food easily while allowing restaurant staff to manage daily operations from one centralized system.

## Expected Outcome

The completed system will simplify the food ordering process, improve order management, reduce manual errors, and provide a better experience for both customers and restaurant administrators.
